# Playtest kit

A 15-minute session with 2 to 4 kids aged 7 to 12, run by a parent or teacher.

## Privacy rules (always)

- No photos, video or voice recordings of children.
- No names: write "Kid A, age 8".
- A parent or teacher is present and agrees first.

## Before

- A laptop or tablet with the game open. Sound on.
- Pick Cadet for ages 7 to 9, Commander for 10 and up.

## During (watch, don't help)

| Moment | Write down |
|---|---|
| Guided first sol | Did they follow the tips? Where did they hesitate? |
| First storm warning | Did they notice it? Did they turn on the shelter? |
| First time a meter goes amber | Did they react before it went red? |
| An expedition finishes | Did they look at the photo? Any reaction? |
| The end | Stars, and whether they want to play again |

## After (ask)

1. What was the hardest choice?
2. Why do you need the radio to Earth?
3. What would you change?
4. Would you play it at school?
